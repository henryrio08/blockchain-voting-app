# Terms of use

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This application is an educational demonstration of a Solidity voting contract and a browser interface.

## Educational use only

Do not use this application to run a real election or to make decisions that require confidential ballots, verified voter identity, voter eligibility, coercion resistance, or professional security review. The contract has not been professionally audited.

## Blockchain transactions

Transactions are sent to the Sepolia network configured by the application. Transactions and wallet addresses are public and may be irreversible. Review the candidate and transaction details before approving a vote in the embedded-wallet flow.

## Gas sponsorship

The application requests gas sponsorship through Alchemy Wallet APIs. Coverage depends on the active third-party policy, contract/call allowlist, and available budget or limits. A request may be rejected. Do not assume that a transaction is sponsored until its status confirms success.

## Third-party services

Privy provides sign-in and embedded-wallet key custody. Alchemy provides blockchain RPC and transaction sponsorship services. These services operate under their own terms and policies. Review authentication prompts and third-party terms before using the app.

## No guarantees

The project is provided for learning and experimentation. It is not a secure voting service and comes without guarantees that it will be available, correct for a particular use, or suitable for production.
